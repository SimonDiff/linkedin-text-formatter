# Privacy policy: DiffStyle for LinkedIn

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

DiffStyle for LinkedIn ("DiffStyle") is a browser extension that adds a text formatting toolbar and a
character counter to LinkedIn's post, comment and message boxes.

## What DiffStyle collects

Nothing. DiffStyle does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.

- The text you format is processed only inside your browser tab, at the moment you click a button or press a shortcut.
  It is never saved and never leaves your device.
- DiffStyle makes no network requests. It has no servers, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies and no ads.
- DiffStyle does not use browser storage, so it keeps no settings, history or identifiers.

## Permissions

DiffStyle runs only on `https://www.linkedin.com/*`, because that is where it shows its toolbar.
It asks for no other permissions.

## Links

The toolbar's About badge links to the author's LinkedIn profile and to the extension's source code on GitHub.
Those sites have their own privacy policies; DiffStyle sends them nothing.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is published, and the date above will change.
